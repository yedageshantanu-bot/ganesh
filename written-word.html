Before I Became Fire

Every fire has an origin. This is mine.
By Ganesh Tajane

Before I Became Fire is the story of a young man whose search for meaning gradually became a journey of awakening. Through the discipline of chess, the silence of the Narmada, moments of doubt, surrender, and profound inner transformation, this book traces the path that slowly dissolved ambition into awareness. It is not a guide, nor a collection of answers—it is an honest account of the experiences that shaped the life before Vayu, before the temple, and before the fire. If these pages leave you with anything, let it be the courage to begin your own search.
