Before I Became Fire

Ganesh Tajane (Expected Release: 6 April 2027)

Before I Became Fire is not the story of enlightenment. It is the story of becoming ready for it. Beginning with a young chess player searching for purpose, the narrative unfolds through years of quiet struggles, sacred encounters, unexpected signs, and moments that could not be explained by logic alone. Every chapter brings the reader closer to the unseen force that would eventually change the course of an ordinary life forever.

"We do not build the fire; we simply clear the hearth. The flame is already there, waiting for the wind of attention."
"In the game of sixty-four squares, I found a language. In the silence between the moves, I found myself."
"The river never taught me where to go; it taught me how to flow. In its silence, I discovered that the destination had never been the point."
