I was trying to find my path. The path was already finding me.

— Ganesh Tajane

Meditation had become a part of my life, but something deeper was beginning to unfold. What started as subtle moments soon became impossible to dismiss. Unexplained coincidences, recurring symbols, powerful dreams, and inner experiences began appearing with unusual frequency. Certain places seemed to call me. Certain books found me at the exact moment I needed them. Questions I carried in silence were often answered before I spoke them aloud. At first, I tried to explain everything logically, telling myself they were coincidences, products of imagination, or the natural result of intense spiritual practice. But the experiences continued. The more I paid attention, the more a quiet pattern emerged—one that seemed to be guiding me towards something I could not yet understand.

I was not seeking Shiva, yet somehow, every path seemed to lead in that direction. Looking back, I can see that these moments were not the destination. They were signposts—gentle nudges from life itself, preparing me for a journey that would change everything. A new chapter was about to begin, and with it, the path of Vayu.

I was not moving
towards Shiva.
Yet everything
that mattered was
moving me towards Him.
